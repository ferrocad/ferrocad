## What the twelve rounds closed

1. **Interaction layer.** The wheel menu (tap-latch/flick), the floating Move form with a
   deferred drag, the origin anchor, sticky gizmo modes, the header Gizmo control, the
   settings-card-as-tool model, the widget controls, the multi-open Dev Tools accordion,
   edition appearance presets, the WCAG gizmo defaults, the always-on-top triad, the
   feathered ribbon stage, filled arrowheads and the grazing-angle fade.
2. **Re-pivot, select mode, fast escalation and rotate rings.** The `Origin` handle and
   snap pipeline, `click_count` → whole-body selection, `Tab`/digit → deferred HUD, and
   the multi-turn/edge-on/detent ring policy.
3. **Gizmo kinds and the set host.** `GizmoKind`, `GizmoSet`, `gizmos_for → Vec`, edition
   composition, the height-grip adapter and the index-aware viewport host.
4. **Part seam.** Re-pivot-independent shape work, the two-profile thread solid, every
   `curve_of` kind (elliptical arc, hyperbola/parabola, Bezier, rational and periodic
   B-spline, offset), the shape-member batch (`isInside`, `hashCode`, `isPartner`,
   `transformGeometry`, `rotated`/`scaled`, `scale`, the tolerance family, `makeWires`,
   `removeInternalWires`, `nullify`, `complement`, `Shape.read`/`Part.read`/`Part.open`,
   `exportStl`, `Part.getFacets`), `getObjectsByLabel`, the `Shape.Orientation` setter,
   `BSplineSurface.toShape()` and the `CenterOfMass` regimes.
5. **Mesh.** `ferrocad_mesh` (payload, STL, operations, `Mesh::Feature`),
   `ferrocad_mesh_py` (`Mesh`, `MeshPart`), `Part.Shape.tessellate`, `BOPTools.SplitAPI`,
   the import-time PySide surface, M1b facet groups, `.bms` and the `.FCStd` mesh member.
6. **Packaging and kernel state.** One combined extension for App + Part + Mesh +
   MeshPart, the packaged-module gate, the `_kernel_id` identity, the `--check-init`
   assertion, the `install()` mixed-image guard, interpreter-keyed Python tables and the
   `Kernel` facade.
7. **Draft.** The Qt-free tool layer, click routing into `draft.pick`/`escape`/`finish`,
   the headless `ViewObject` style properties, the toggleable snap bar, and M3's first
   slice (`getEditorMode`/`setEditorMode`, `Placement.isIdentity`/`move`, `Matrix`
   copy/multiply, `PropertyVectorDistance`, strict `addObject`).
8. **SimpleCAD.** S0/S1 audit and baseline, the 88-module image, the S2–S3 measured
   baseline (702/44/368/2), the identity/query/representation frontier and the
   fillet/chamfer build guard.
9. **Shell and docs.** The 10k-line `shell.rs` split into surface modules, the
   collapsed-drawer fix, the `compile_with` diagnostic paths, the generated API/docs
   checks and the separate `ferrocad/docs` Pages repository.
