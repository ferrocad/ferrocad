## What landed

- **Serializers F1–F8.** `.ferrocad` (native JSON) and `.FCStd` are now parallel,
  independent backends; `saveAs(.FCStd)` writes the real ZIP container, and the reader
  restores shapes, shape lists, expressions and the GUI document.
- **Lineage S1–S6c.** `ferrocad_lineage` owns the payload-generic vocabulary and policy;
  Part stores the map, resolves through it, exposes `ElementReverseMap`, persists it in
  both formats (the `PartShapeN.lineage` sidecar), and reproduces upstream's **20/47**
  compound-cut maps. `ElementMapVersion` stays off.
- **Links L0–L5.** `isLink`/`getLinkedObject`/`getElementIndex`, dotted `getSubObject`
  paths, `App::Link*` containers and elements, non-uniform scale, and lineage-backed
  sub-element resolution.
- **Qt reduction Q0a–Q3b.** The pure-Python PySide shim, the `.ui` reader, the
  `PySideUic` proxies, the gpui form renderer, and the shell-hosted `FormDialog`.
- **The shadow producer** (stage bounds → the fixed program ABI), **SimpleCAD S0** (the
  conformance group and the static audit), and a narrow **`Draft` shim** that removed
  the two Part-conformance load errors without shadowing upstream Draft.
- **The complete Part surface:** constructors and overloads, conics and NURBS
  conversion, `ShapeFix`, `ChFi2d`, `Sketcher::SketchObject`, `Body`/`Mirroring`
  shapes, torus/wedge/revolution/helix/thread geometry, live `Shape.Placement`, and
  upstream's cyclic-expression wording.
- **All eight link-array failures** closed; the four files are 24/24.
